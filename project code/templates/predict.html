Parameters Explained

Total & Direct Bilirubin

Bilirubin is created by breakdown of red blood cells. High levels indicate liver dysfunction or bile duct blockage.

Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP)

An enzyme related to bile ducts. High levels indicate blocked ducts or bone problems.

ALT & AST

Intracellular enzymes. High concentrations indicate liver injury (hepatitis, toxins, cirrhosis).

Albumin & Total Protein

Synthesized solely by liver. Lower levels indicate loss of liver synthetic tissue capacity (cirrhosis indicator).
